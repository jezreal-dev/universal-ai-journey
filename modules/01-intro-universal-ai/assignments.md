## 📝 Problem Formulations & Solution Verification

### 📌 Problem 1 — Machine Learning Paradigm Taxonomy

#### Question Formulation
Classify the following three AI scenarios into Supervised Learning, Unsupervised Learning, or Reinforcement Learning:
1. Predicting house prices using historical sales data with known square footage and locations.
2. Grouping customer purchase behaviors into distinct market segments without pre-existing labels.
3. Training an autonomous trading bot to maximize long-term portfolio returns via market action rewards.

#### Verified Solution & Explanation
* **1. Supervised Learning**: Input features ($X$) map to continuous target prices ($y$).
* **2. Unsupervised Learning**: Finding hidden latent structures and clusters without labeled targets.
* **3. Reinforcement Learning**: Agent interacting with an environment via action-reward feedback loops.
