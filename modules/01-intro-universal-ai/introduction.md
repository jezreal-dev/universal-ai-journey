## Official Course Content

### Module Overview
The **Introduction to Universal AI** module sets the stage for the entire program. It explains why AI fluency matters in today’s rapidly evolving technological landscape and outlines the goals of the Universal AI curriculum.  

Key points:
- AI has been transforming society for decades, but the pace of change accelerated dramatically with the rise of generative AI (e.g., ChatGPT in 2022).  
- The world needs adaptable, AI‑fluent professionals who can navigate cross‑disciplinary challenges.  
- Universal AI is designed to provide a **holistic understanding of AI**, not just narrow technical skills that may quickly become obsolete.  

### Learning Goals
Learners are expected to:
- Understand the scope of AI and its impact across industries.  
- Recognize the foundational modules that build AI fluency.  
- Prepare for deeper exploration through articles, lectures, and knowledge checks.  
- Develop the ability to assess, embrace, and master emerging AI technologies.  

---

## Program Pathways
The 15 foundational modules are organized into five suggested learning pathways:  
1. Fundamentals of Programming and Machine Learning  
2. Fundamentals of Deep Learning  
3. Fundamentals of Prescriptive AI and Decision‑Making  
4. Fundamentals of Large Language Models (LLM)  
5. Fundamentals of Explainability and AI Ethics  

These pathways allow learners to enter at the right level for their background and build conceptual depth in distinct areas of AI.
