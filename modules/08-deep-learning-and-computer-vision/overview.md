# Module 8: Deep Learning and Computer Vision

## 🎯 Module Overview & Learning Goals

### Overview
Welcome to **Deep Learning and Computer Vision**!

In this module, you’ll explore how neural networks learn to recognize patterns and make predictions from both structured and unstructured data.

We’ll start with the core ingredients of neural networks: perceptrons, activation functions, and layered connections that transform simple computations into powerful, non-linear models. You’ll see how training through backpropagation and gradient-based optimization turns these networks into adaptive learners, and how techniques like dropout, weight decay, and early stopping prevent them from memorizing noise instead of generalizing to new data.

From there, we’ll shift to computer vision, where convolutional neural networks (CNNs) uncover spatial hierarchies in images, detecting everything from edges to full objects. You’ll also learn how transfer learning enables us to adapt pre-trained models to new tasks, dramatically reducing the need for large labeled datasets.

By the end, you’ll understand how deep learning architectures—from basic perceptrons to CNNs—form the backbone of modern AI applications, from healthcare imaging to autonomous driving.

---

### 🎯 Learning Goals
By the end of this module, learners will be able to:
1. **Perceptrons & Architectures**: Explain how perceptrons, activation functions, and hidden layers combine to create powerful neural network architectures.
2. **Structured vs. Unstructured Inputs**: Compare structured inputs (e.g., tabular datasets) with unstructured inputs (e.g., images) and describe how models process each.
3. **Regularization & Generalization**: Discuss strategies for controlling underfitting and overfitting, including regularization techniques such as dropout, weight decay, and early stopping.
4. **CNNs & Transfer Learning**: Describe how convolutional layers extract spatial features from images and how transfer learning adapts pre-trained models to new tasks.
5. **Real-World Scaling**: Summarize how the fundamental components of neural networks scale to support modern deep learning applications.
