## Module Overview

In this module, we explore how neural networks learn to make predictions across both structured and unstructured data.

We begin with perceptrons, the building blocks of neural networks, and see how they model structured data like tables of patient records or financial transactions. Extending perceptrons into multilayer networks allows us to capture non-linear patterns while balancing underfitting and overfitting. We then shift to unstructured data (such as images and text) where convolutional layers and embeddings uncover spatial structure and semantic meaning that hand-crafted features often miss.

By the end of this module, we will understand how neural networks adapt to different types of data, forming the backbone of modern AI applications in healthcare, finance, and beyond.

---

## Learning Goals

By the end of this module, learners will be able to:
- **Describe how perceptrons and multilayer networks form the basis of neural network predictors.**
- **Distinguish between structured data** (e.g., tables) and **unstructured data** (e.g., images, text).
- **Explain how neural networks handle underfitting, overfitting, and generalization.**
- **Understand the role of embeddings** in representing unstructured data.
- **Summarize the key building blocks** that make neural networks flexible models for different data types.
