## Official Course Content

### Module Summary
Congratulations on completing **Module 0 of Universal AI**!  

This module provided a broad, accessible introduction to artificial intelligence (AI): what it is, how it works, and how it is already transforming the world.  

Across the articles and lectures, you explored:
- The evolution of AI from early rule‑based systems to modern machine learning, deep learning, and large language models (LLMs).  
- How AI agents perceive the world, reason about what they see, and act on those decisions through a continuous loop supported by data, knowledge, and computational models.  
- How machine learning models are trained, why data is split into training, validation, and test sets, and how generative AI systems create text, images, and other content.  
- Real‑world applications of AI across healthcare, education, and agriculture — improving diagnosis, streamlining decisions, personalizing learning, and supporting food security.  
- Challenges and misconceptions surrounding AI, including bias, explainability, and the limits of model performance.  

Together, the articles and lectures built a clear foundation for understanding both the mechanics of AI systems and their real‑world impact.  

With this grounding, you are now well prepared to continue your journey through Universal AI and dive deeper into the techniques, applications, and ethics shaping our world.
