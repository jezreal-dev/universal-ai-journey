## Official Course Content

### Introduction
Artificial intelligence (AI) has been changing the way we live, work, study, and operate for over 50 years. With the launch of ChatGPT and the introduction of generative AI into the public discourse in November 2022, the pace of change and advancement of the technology has increased exponentially.  

The world needs adaptable, AI‑fluent professionals ready to navigate this rapidly evolving technological landscape. This means rethinking how we equip learners with a holistic understanding of AI and its impact on all fields and industries.  

MIT Open Learning created **Universal AI** to help learners develop fluency in artificial intelligence and gain a deep understanding of the core theories, concepts, and problem‑solving methods that define the field. With this broad fluency, rather than limited skills that may quickly become obsolete, learners will be equipped to assess, embrace, and master emerging AI technologies as they evolve.

### Instructor Transcript (Excerpt)
> “Universal AI is part of Open Learning educational offerings with which we aspire to reach 1 billion learners.”  
> — Dimitris Bertsimas, Vice Provost for Open Learning, MIT  

Dimitris Bertsimas explained that MIT traditionally reaches only 11,000 students — “1 in a million” of the global population. Universal AI is part of a moonshot to educate **1 billion learners in 10 years**, using accessible pedagogy and personalization powered by AI.  

He emphasized that today’s challenges (AI, climate change, healthcare, manufacturing, biology) cut across disciplines, so education must adapt to foster interdisciplinary thinkers. Universal AI is designed to be applied, practical, and scalable, supported by AI tutors like **AskTIM**.
