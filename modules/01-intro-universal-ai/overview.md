## 🌟 Executive Summary

Welcome to **Introduction to Universal AI**, the foundational module of the MIT Universal AI Track. This module establishes the core philosophy, technical roadmap, and societal implications of artificial intelligence—bridging statistical learning, neural architectures, optimization theory, and responsible deployment.

---

## 🎯 Key Learning Goals

1. **AI Ecosystem Orientation**: Navigate the core domains of AI—from supervised and unsupervised learning to prescriptive optimization, multimodal systems, and agentic LLMs.
2. **Foundational Technical Literacy**: Understand the mathematical principles underpinning machine learning algorithms and computational graphs.
3. **Ethical & Societal Awareness**: Evaluate AI's impact on human labor, decision governance, and algorithmic fairness.
