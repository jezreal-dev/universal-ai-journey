# Module 6: Foundations of Neural Networks — Conclusion & Summary

This module covered how neural networks handle both structured (tabular) and unstructured (image, text) data. We explored perceptrons and multilayer perceptrons as predictors, and saw how representation learning enables models to capture complex patterns directly from raw inputs.

## Key Takeaways:
1. **Perceptrons and MLPs build predictors for structured data**: Learned how to build simple and deep networks to classify tabular data.
2. **Empirical error vs. true error highlights generalization**: Evaluated models using training/testing datasets to detect generalization performance.
3. **Underfitting and overfitting require balance and regularization**: Explored capacity tuning, early stopping, and weight decay to balance complex models.
4. **CNNs and embeddings capture structure in unstructured data**: Utilized spatial networks (CNNs) and low-dimensional spaces (embeddings) for complex files like image arrays.
5. **Representation learning turns raw inputs into useful features**: Utilized techniques like PCA and Autoencoders to learn compact features that remove noise and speed up training.

Congratulations on completing Module 6! You now have the foundations of neural network modeling, equipping you to analyze structured and unstructured datasets and understand how to build predictive models effectively.
