# Module 9: Data-Driven Prescriptive AI — Overview

## 📌 Module Overview
Welcome to **Data-Driven Prescriptive AI**! This module explores how to move beyond prediction to actionable prescriptions using data-driven methods. You will learn how to design decision policies that directly optimize outcomes, leveraging techniques such as policy trees, prescriptive models built on machine learning predictions, and prescriptive neural networks. We will examine the theory, algorithms, and practical applications that enable prescriptive analytics to bridge the gap between prediction and decision-making.

Each lecture comes with a downloadable PDF slide deck mapping directly to visual materials shown in the video, providing an alternative study aid for text, math, and graphics details.

---

## 🎯 Learning Goals
By the end of this module, learners will be able to:
* **Distinguish Analytics Paradigms**: Understand the fundamental difference between predictive analytics (what will happen) and prescriptive analytics (what action to take).
* **Derive Data-Driven Decision Policies**: Explain how optimal decision rules and policies can be learned directly from empirical data.
* **Master Policy Trees**: Describe the algorithmic concept, objective formulation, and structure of policy trees for optimal treatment assignment.
* **Integrate ML into Optimization**: Combine machine learning predictive outputs (counterfactual estimation) into prescriptive optimization frameworks.
* **Architect Prescriptive Neural Networks**: Understand the architecture, loss formulation, and end-to-end training process for prescriptive neural networks.
* **Solve Real-World Decision Problems**: Apply prescriptive AI techniques to complex decision-making scenarios across healthcare, personalized pricing, resource allocation, and operations.

## 📝 Official Module Summary

In this module, we explored the transformation from predictive insights to actionable prescriptions. We examined how policy trees can formalize decision-making and adapt to different contexts, how predictive ML outputs can be embedded into prescriptive frameworks, and how prescriptive neural networks can handle high-dimensional and complex decision spaces.

### Key Takeaways:
* **Prediction vs. Prescription**: The fundamental distinction between forecasting outcomes (predictive analytics) and optimizing decision actions under operational constraints (prescriptive analytics).
* **Interpretable Policy Trees**: How policy trees represent transparent, data-driven decision rules mapping input features directly to optimal treatments.
* **Combining Predictive ML & Optimization**: Techniques like $OP^2T$ for combining predictive machine learning models with prescriptive decision-making, including Rejection Learning and Fairness Auditing.
* **Prescriptive Neural Networks (PNNs)**: The design, Softmax loss formulation, and empirical benefits of PNNs and Mirrored OCTs for solving complex, continuous, and multimodal decision problems.
